🤔 What is an idiom?
🌧️🐱🐶
"It's raining cats and dogs"

An idiom is a phrase or expression that is playful, expressive, creative, and sometimes silly — something that people in a culture use with each other.

An idiom can be confusing to people learning English because you often cannot translate it exactly. "It's raining cats and dogs" means "It's raining a lot." It does not mean animals are literally falling from the sky!

Why do different cultures have idioms? Maybe to make talking more interesting — a more fun, visual way to talk about everyday things.
